Founder's Message

Mazhar Durrani

Founder — Durrani Group of Companies

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Durrani Group of Companies.

Coming from a respected Durrani family of Charsadda, my journey has been built on dedication, trust, and continuous learning. I started my professional career in the real estate sector in Dubai, UAE, where I gained valuable experience in property markets, customer relations, marketing strategies, and international real estate practices.

After gaining extensive knowledge and experience, I returned to Pakistan with a vision to bring professional standards and a customer-focused approach to the real estate industry. I began my journey in Peshawar's real estate sector from Nasir Bagh Road, working with commitment, honesty, and a passion for creating value for our clients.

Over the years, through hard work and strong relationships, Durrani Group of Companies has grown into a trusted name in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with diverse businesses including Durrani Real Estate, Durrani Builders, Durrani Developers Pvt. Ltd., Durrani Marketing Agency, Durrani Garden Landscape, Spring Rose Real Estate and Spring Rose Events.

Our mission is not only to develop properties but to build lasting relationships based on trust, quality, transparency, and excellence.

At Durrani Group, we believe that every project represents a dream, and every client deserves commitment, honesty, and professional service. We continue to work towards creating modern developments and opportunities that contribute positively to our community and future generations.

I sincerely thank our clients, partners, and team members for their trust and support. With the blessings of Allah Almighty and the dedication of our team, we look forward to achieving greater milestones in the years ahead.

"Building Trust. Creating Futures."

Mazhar Durrani — Founder
Durrani Group of Companies
