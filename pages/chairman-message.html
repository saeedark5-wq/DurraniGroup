CEO's Message

Mubashir Durrani

CEO — Durrani Group of Companies

I am honored to welcome you to Durrani Group of Companies, a name built on trust, dedication, and commitment.

Belonging to a respected Durrani family of Charsadda, my journey in the real estate sector started at an early stage of my career. With a passion for property development, marketing, and serving people, I entered the real estate industry in Pakistan and gained valuable experience in understanding market trends, customer needs, and investment opportunities.

Starting my professional journey with a newly established real estate venture on Nasir Bagh Road, Peshawar, I worked with determination, honesty, and a clear vision to create a reliable name in the property sector. Through continuous effort, strong relationships, and dedication to excellence, I have had the privilege of becoming a recognized personality in the real estate community of Peshawar and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Today, under the umbrella of Durrani Group of Companies, we proudly operate across multiple sectors: Durrani Real Estate, Durrani Builders, Durrani Developers Pvt. Ltd., Durrani Marketing Agency, Durrani Garden Landscape, Spring Rose Real Estate and Spring Rose Events.

Our vision is to provide professional real estate solutions, develop quality projects, and create opportunities that bring value to our clients and communities.

At Durrani Group, we believe that success is built on integrity, innovation, teamwork, and long-term relationships. Our commitment is to maintain the highest standards of professionalism while continuing to grow and contribute to the development of our region.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to our clients, partners, and team members for their trust and confidence. With dedication and the support of our valued community, we look forward to reaching new milestones.

"Building Trust. Creating Futures."

Mubashir Durrani — CEO
Durrani Group of Companies
