Owning a Home Has Never Been Easier: Installment Plans Explained

By Mubashir Durrani — CEO, Durrani Group of Companies • 4 min read

One of the proudest moments of my career is seeing a family receive the keys to a home they paid for in small monthly installments — not with a lump sum they spent years saving. Installment plans have transformed the Peshawar property market, and they are the reason home ownership is no longer reserved for the wealthy.

How a 3-Year Installment Plan Works

A typical plan in our projects works like this: a booking payment of around 25% of the total price, followed by equal monthly installments over 2–4 years, with possession and transfer upon completion of payment. The math is simple — for example, a 20-lac plot with a 5-lac down payment works out to about 41,000 PKR per month over 3 years. Our website’s calculator shows this for any budget.

Why Installment Plans Benefit Both Sides

For the buyer, the monthly burden is manageable, and the plot is secured at today’s price — so even if market rates rise during the plan, your investment rises with it. For the society, steady payments fund the development of roads, utilities and amenities, which raises the value of every member’s plot. It is a partnership where everyone wins.

Key Takeaways

  • Booking is typically ~25% down, then easy monthly installments
  • Your price is locked at booking — market growth works for you
  • Installments fund development, raising the value of your plot
  • Always get the full payment plan in writing from the society office

What to Watch For

Not all installment plans are equal. Confirm that installments go to the society’s official account, that every payment is receipted, and that the plan states what happens if you need to extend or transfer. A transparent plan in writing protects you for years.
