Investing from Abroad: A 2026 Guide for Overseas Pakistanis

By Mazhar Durrani — Founder, Durrani Group of Companies • 5 min read

I spent years working in Dubai's property market before returning to Peshawar, so I know exactly what an overseas Pakistani feels: you have the money, you have the intention, but you are thousands of miles away and terrified of being cheated. The good news is that 2026 is the best year yet for overseas investors — if you follow the right process.

The Overseas Advantage

Property in Peshawar still offers entry prices that are a fraction of Dubai, Islamabad or Karachi, while the appreciation potential in developing sectors like Regi Model Town and DHA Peshawar is among the strongest in the country. For a family earning in dirhams, pounds or dollars, a well-chosen plot is one of the safest stores of value you can own.

How to Buy Safely From Abroad

  1. Use a verified society. Confirm the NOC status with the concerned authority, not just with the seller.
  2. Deal in writing. A proper allotment letter and payment receipts issued by the society office — nothing less.
  3. Use official channels. Bank transfers keep a clear record for tax purposes and for the society’s records.
  4. Appoint a trusted representative. A reputable agent who can visit the site, check documents and send you real updates.

Key Takeaways

  • Peshawar entry prices still beat most major cities in Pakistan
  • Always buy with the society office, not a middleman’s file
  • Bank transfers protect you and simplify the paperwork
  • Work with a team that sends you verifiable updates
