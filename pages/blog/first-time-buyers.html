5 Mistakes First-Time Property Buyers Make in Peshawar

By Mubashir Durrani — CEO, Durrani Group of Companies • 6 min read

In two decades of dealing in Peshawar property, I have watched the same mistakes repeat themselves year after year — and they cost families real money. If you are buying your first plot or house, read this before you pay anything.

Mistake 1: Buying a File Without Verifying the Society

The biggest one, by far. A "bargain" file from an unverified society is not a bargain — it is a lottery ticket. Before any payment, verify the NOC with the authority and visit the site yourself. We published the full checklist in our verification guide for a reason.

Mistake 2: Chasing "Cheapest Rate in Town"

If a rate is dramatically below market, there is a reason — and it is rarely a good one. In verified societies like Regi Model Town and DHA Peshawar, prices are tightly correlated with development progress. A below-market rate usually means below-market legitimacy.

Mistake 3: Paying the Agent Instead of the Society

Your payments must go to the society’s official bank account with a receipt issued on society letterhead. Money paid to an individual — agent or not — creates a paper trail that can cost you years and lacs if something goes wrong.

Mistake 4: Ignoring the Payment Plan Terms

First-time buyers often focus only on the monthly amount and never read what happens with late payments, transfers or resale mid-plan. A plan you don’t understand is a plan that can surprise you. Get everything in writing.

Mistake 5: Buying Without a Visit — Even From Abroad

Overseas buyers, I understand the distance — but the solution is not skipping the visit. It is having someone you trust walk the site, check the office, and video-call you through it. Never rely on screenshots alone.

The Golden Rules

  • Verify the NOC — always, with the authority itself
  • Pay the society, not the agent, and keep every receipt
  • Visit the site yourself or through a trusted representative
  • Understand every line of the payment plan before signing
