Plots vs Houses: Where Should You Invest in Peshawar?

By Mazhar Durrani — Founder, Durrani Group of Companies • 4 min read

"Should I buy a plot or a house?" I answer this question almost every week, and the honest answer is: it depends on your goal. In Peshawar’s 2026 market, plots and houses serve completely different purposes. Let me break down how I guide my own clients.

Investing for Growth: Buy a Plot

Plots in developing societies — Regi Model Town, DHA Peshawar, and approved schemes in the Hayatabad belt — historically appreciate faster than finished houses because the "development premium" is still being earned. A 10-marla plot bought early in a developing sector has outperformed most finished-home purchases in my 20 years in this market. You also have lower entry cost, and you can build when you’re ready.

Investing for Income or Immediate Use: Buy a House

If you want rental income today, or you want to move in, a ready or near-ready house makes sense. In Peshawar, demand for finished homes in gated communities is strong, especially among families returning from abroad and young professionals. The trade-off is a higher entry price and slower appreciation than early-stage plots.

Key Takeaways

  • Plots = higher appreciation potential, lower entry cost
  • Houses = rental income and immediate use, higher entry price
  • Mixed approach: buy a plot now, build when the market rewards it
  • Never buy "bargain" plots in unapproved sectors — that’s not investing, it’s gambling

My Recommendation for Most Buyers

If you already own a home, buy a plot. If you don’t own a home yet, buy the plot first and build when you can — the plot protects your money while it grows. The only wrong answer is buying in a society you cannot verify. Everything else is timing and patience.
