Regi Model Town: Peshawar's Smartest Investment in 2026

By Mazhar Durrani — Founder, Durrani Group of Companies • 6 min read

Every market has a moment when it shifts from "promising" to "proven." For Peshawar, that moment is now — and Regi Model Town is at the centre of it. As someone who has dealt in this city's property market for over two decades, I can tell you the difference between hype and real, sustainable growth. Regi Model Town is the latter.

Why the Market is Moving Toward Regi

The government's commitment to the new city district is no longer just a promise on paper. Infrastructure work, road networks and utility connections are visibly progressing. In my experience, property prices follow infrastructure with a lag of 12–24 months — which means the investors who enter now are buying before the next wave of appreciation, not after it.

Where the Growth Still Is

One mistake buyers make is assuming the whole of Regi Model Town has already "happened." It hasn't. The most affordable entry points — residential plots in well-located sectors with verified NOC status — are still available at prices that made sense last year, not at the rates this market will justify once development completes.

Key Takeaways

  • Regi Model Town growth is infrastructure-driven, not hype-driven
  • Entry prices are still 12–24 months behind infrastructure completion
  • Verified, NOC-approved files are the only files worth buying
  • Early sectors deliver the best price-to-potential ratio

What I Tell Every Serious Investor

Buy land where you can see the development yourself. Visit the site. Check the NOC. Ask about the payment plan in writing. And never, ever buy a file that cannot be verified at the society office. A plot in the right sector of Regi Model Town, bought properly, is one of the strongest long-term assets a Pakistani family can hold in 2026.
