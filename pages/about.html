Who We Are

A Name Built on Trust & Quality

Durrani Group of Companies is a premier business group headquartered in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. What began as a professional real estate venture on Nasir Bagh Road has grown into seven companies serving thousands of families.

Our founder, Mazhar Durrani, brought international experience from Dubai's property market to raise the standard of real estate dealing in the region. Under the leadership of CEO Mubashir Durrani, the group has expanded into construction, development, marketing, landscaping and events.
