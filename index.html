Pakistan's Premier Real Estate & Development Group

Founded by Mazhar Durrani and led by CEO Mubashir Durrani, Durrani Group of Companies has grown into a trusted name in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — operating across real estate, construction, development, marketing, and landscaping.

From Nasir Bagh Road, Peshawar, to international standards of service, we deliver professional property solutions for plots, houses, and commercial developments across Peshawar, DHA Peshawar, Regi Model Town, Hayatabad, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi.

Founder's Message

Building Dreams on a Foundation of Trust

Coming from a respected Durrani family of Charsadda, my journey began in Dubai's real estate sector, where I gained valuable experience in property markets and international practices. I returned to Pakistan with a vision to bring professional standards and a customer-focused approach to the industry — starting on Nasir Bagh Road, Peshawar.

Our mission is not only to develop properties but to build lasting relationships based on trust, quality, transparency, and excellence. Every project represents a dream, and every client deserves commitment, honesty, and professional service.

"Building Trust. Creating Futures."

Mazhar Durrani — Founder
